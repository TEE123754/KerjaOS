# M12 owner reminders and optional account email

React/Vite + FastAPI + Supabase Postgres. No Redis, Resend dependency, paid service, third-party analytics or model added. No employer message is generated. Apply additive `20261005120000_m12_owned_reminders.sql` after backed-up M1–M11 migration/isolated restore. No remote migration/deployment was performed.

## In-app baseline and dates

Sign in → Reminders → Load reminders. Create personal/follow_up/preparation with title (120 printable characters), explicit ISO timestamp including UTC offset, and an IANA display timezone. UTC is stored; timezone is display only, so the two New York DST 01:30 values with -04:00/-05:00 identify different instants. Offset is explicit to avoid inferring ambiguous local time. Due must be no earlier than now minus one minute (submission latency) and no later than 366 days. In-app due/upcoming uses server refresh time, not a continuously running notification promise. Optional owned internal/manual/discovery UUID links are verified at creation and dispatch; personal origin has no reference. Maximum 200 reminders, 50/page, latest 100 history events. Load more, snooze using a new ISO instant, complete/cancel/forget with current revision and idempotency key. Stale edits fail; refresh before retry. Actions never change hiring stages/statuses. Draft titles survive taskbar/minimize and guard page leave; no raw text in browser persistence.

Follow-up/preparation rules cancel on linked withdrawal/rejection/hire/closure. Explicit personal tasks remain until acted upon. Manual/discovery states remain self-reported. Forget of manual/saved/source notes removes linked reminders, history and deliveries, including personal linked reminders. Confirmed/completed M8 human bookings suppress/cancel duplicate internal preparation reminders; link to existing Interview notices/ICS. Personal custom preparation tasks can still be independently recorded as personal. Candidate B and foreign/MFA staff cannot access another candidate's reminders/preferences/history.

## Email preference, preview and truthful states

Default in-app only: REMINDER_WORKER_ENABLED=false, REMINDER_SENDER=disabled, SQL reminder_policy enabled=false/mode=disabled. Opt-in requires verified Auth account email, versioned preference and CSRF. Preview is generic with no title, job/company, resume, private note, credit/criminal/identity data or employer contact. Sender gets recipient only from service-authorized Auth metadata; browser cannot supply addresses. Unsubscribe via signed-in preference button; opt-out cancels queued/leased/failed work. A message already at dispatch may arrive, which the UI states. No hidden recipient or employer follow-up automation.

Statuses: not_queued (worker not run/no opt-in), expired_window (>7 days overdue, no catch-up email), disabled (policy/adapter unavailable), queued, leased, sending, sent (SMTP accepted, not proof of inbox delivery), fixture (synthetic receipt, no email), failed (pre-dispatch/quota wait), dead_letter (bounded failure), unknown (uncertain SMTP/crashed dispatch, no auto retry), cancelled. Disabled/cancelled/unknown occurrences do not reactivate after mode/preference changes. Explicit snooze creates a new revision occurrence; no automatic resend for old occurrences. Do not use snooze to bypass an unknown delivery without reviewing it.

## Bounded worker and delivery safety

Run `backend/scripts/reminders_m12.py` once with server-only settings. At most 50 enqueued and 20 processed (hard cap 50) per invocation. Work is keyed by reminder_id+revision, private outbox pattern independent of recruitment outbox. Leases 2 minutes; expired unstarted lease can be reclaimed up to 3 attempts. Preflight failure waits 15 minutes; third failure dead-letters. Unknown/send boundary cannot return to queued. Bounded claim can stop on an exhausted row; next invocation continues remaining work. No always-on process or recurring automation installed.

Dispatch takes global quota then owner advisory lock and reminder/outbox locks; rechecks policy, revision, active due state, owned link, closed outcomes, current opt-in and verified recipient. DB policy mode must match adapter; mismatch completes disabled without sending. Authorize at most 3 owner/30 global UTC-day dispatches by default (operator bounds 10/100). Quota wait is until next UTC day and counts attempts. Dispatch reserves a durable occurrence ledger before network send; duplicate calls, restart and old backup replay cannot reauthorize it. SMTP Message-ID is stable metadata, not a provider idempotency guarantee. SMTP ambiguous errors mark unknown; no network retry. TTL/receipt counters are metadata only, never recipient addresses/body logs. Failed completion leaves sending; lease expiry becomes unknown.

Concurrency cannot recall an already authorized network dispatch: preference/cancellation/kill after the final DB check may race an in-flight send. UI discloses this. After opt-out returns, future authorizations are denied; pre-dispatch stale revisions/leases cannot send. Hosted multi-connection/SMTP ambiguity tests remain required. A single local PGlite transaction engine does not prove those races.

## Privacy, retention, backup and rollback

Owned portability adds reminders, reminder_history, reminder_deliveries and reminder_preferences; 100/page, excludes delivery lease/recipient/receipt details. Notices EN/BM version privacy-demo-v4 explain opt-out/in-flight limitation. Private workspace erasure removes old reminder text/history/outbox and opts out preferences under reviewed cutoff; newer data requires operator reconciliation. Forget/cleanup scrubs stored operation-key title results, preserves removal tombstones. One bounded `retention_m12.py` removes up to 100 terminal reminders older than 90 days and up to 100 old history/terminal delivery rows each; active reminders are bounded, not silently deleted. Delivery attempts older than seven days are not enqueued. Text/history lifetimes are demo policy, not a reviewed legal promise.

Minimal reminder removal, opt-out and dispatch-boundary ledgers remain service-only for restore and quota/deduplication; no email/title/body. Their retention/cutoff/compaction is TBC-LEGAL/release review, not covered by the 90-day text cleanup claim. They remain after reminder removal to prevent quota reset/resend. Full account disposition stays reviewed/manual.

M9 recovery now seals these newest ledgers with erasure/document/tracker ledgers. Preserve the newest archive independently of older backup. Replay removal/opt-out/dispatch boundaries with workers/senders disabled on isolated restore target before traffic; sending outcomes become unknown and existing occurrence rows cannot be retried. Apply missing M12 additive migration in old restore before scripts. Limit replay to 10,000 reviewed ledger entries; larger reconciliation requires explicit operator batch design, never silently truncated. Ordinary post-backup snoozes/preferences/business/consent changes still require reconciliation. Do not claim complete automatic recovery from an old archive alone. Count-only monitor exposes unknown/dead-letter and overdue in-app reminders.

Rollback: REMINDER_WORKER_ENABLED=false plus service policy enabled=false/mode disabled; preserve in-app reminders/history/ownership, cancel pre-dispatch queued work through reviewed preference/state checks if stopping permanently, retain dedupe/opt-out/deletion ledgers and privacy exports/cleanup. No destructive rollback, insecure grants or old-send retry.
