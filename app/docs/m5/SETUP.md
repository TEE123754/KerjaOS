# M5 — background evidence, manual review and synthetic checks

This is a free workflow implementation, not official CTOS/CCRIS/police integration. CTOS Basic, CCRIS and criminal evidence have separate purpose/version consent and coverage labels. The existing legacy graph/paid provider paths remain disabled. No new model/service/dependency is needed. Stack: FastAPI/Pydantic, PostgreSQL Supabase RPC/RLS/Storage, encrypted Fernet reports, React/TypeScript/Vite; existing pytest/PGlite/Playwright. Frontend Vercel/backend Railway/database Supabase remain selected but undeployed here.

## Setup / synthetic demo

After the configured M1–M4 backup/restore/live gates, apply `20261002150000_m5_background_evidence.sql` in order. No remote migration or live data was used. An assigned active HM/admin with MFA configures a **new-application** job policy in Background evidence. Choose each necessary typed check, a role justification (15–1000 characters) and mock/manual/unconfigured official method. Stage is fixed after quiz (P4). Admin alone may authorize a per-check exception; ordinary HM cannot. Configure before candidates apply; existing application snapshots are not silently rewritten. Legal activation is false whenever a new policy is saved.

Candidate reaches P4 after required identity/quiz and separate human advancement, selects a check and explicit employer/application/purpose/version consent. Start an ambiguous synthetic mock. Case is queued independently of the request; assigned HM can process one local demo item in the UI, or operator runs `backend/scripts/background_m5.py` to claim one eligible item. Mock result is labelled SIMULATION / synthetic_workflow_only and never an official clearance. Ambiguity puts the application on hold. Candidate explains/disputes without portal/account/identity numbers. HM personally reviews against role necessity and confirms consideration of the explanation. This records evidence only; a separate human resume/advance/shortlist/reject action is required. Dispute after shortlist/interview entry also puts the application on hold, revokes derived evidence and blocks further progression until reviewed and explicitly resumed.

For manual demo, choose manual in the job policy before apply and upload an **existing synthetic PDF**, at most 3 MB/20 pages, no automatic PDF actions. It stays encrypted in the private bucket, quarantined and labelled candidate_supplied_unverified / manual_document_only. No LLM/OCR parser, portal credentials, cookie scraping, new paid CGC/MyCTOS purchase or broad debt/offence score. Download tickets last 60 seconds, bind user/session, and recheck role/assignment/consent/expiry on every download. A PDF format check is not malware scanning or certified authenticity. Real PDFs remain blocked in the API; future approved code release plus DB global/per-policy legal activation and qualified operations are required. Supplying service keys does not authorize real reports.

An official provider stub always returns unavailable / not_configured / no_check_performed, regardless of simulated scenario. It cannot be reviewed as satisfied or adverse. Only an admin with an explicitly snapshotted exception may approve an exception, recording that no check occurred. Exceptions remain visibly distinct. A credit gate with CTOS Basic **and** CCRIS requires independently reviewed evidence for both. Generic/unbound legacy evidence cannot satisfy either. Poor credit does not become a ranking feature; background-driven rejection requires referenced, human-reviewed concern evidence and a role-specific reason. Unavailability alone cannot authorize rejection.

## Worker / consent / retention

Each worker operation claims a two-minute lease via SKIP LOCKED, increments attempts and accepts only normalized needs_review/evidence_consistent/unavailable codes. No webhook endpoint exists; external vendor/webhook activation (and signature/replay tests) remains deferred. Three expired failed leases exhaust into dead_letter / unavailable. Completion is token/expiry checked and replay-safe; official-success and fabricated clear codes are rejected by SQL. Cancellation on withdrawal/rejection/job closure or consent revocation stops pending work, revokes derived evidence and expires raw reports immediately. A late result is recorded in a private minimal quarantine ledger; it cannot satisfy screening or overwrite a cancelled case.

Run `backend/scripts/background_m5.py --cleanup` as a bounded local scheduled operation when credentials are configured. It deletes actual encrypted objects before token-checked metadata/ledger completion; a crash is retried after lease expiry. Raw report expiry: at most 24 hours, shortened to one hour after review, immediate after cancellation/revocation/replacement. At most three reports per application/day. Overdue encrypted objects or stranded upload intents block new capture until cleanup; pending uploads are held until complete or stranded for an hour to prevent delete/write races. M1 generic retention excludes identity/background objects; their specialized lease/ledger workers own deletion. Keep reviewed minimal evidence/history per reviewed release retention policy, not raw documents forever. Demonstrate backup restoration plus deletion-ledger replay before real use.

Rollback: privileged operator sets `kerja_private.background_policy.dispatch_enabled=false` to pause new dispatch (existing processing/history/cleanup remains available); raw real capture is already off. Preserve consent/dispute/history and continue due purges. Do not drop new tables or restore revoked/deleted evidence as valid.

Live gates deferred: Auth/PostgREST wrapper grants/isolation, true multi-connection lease/closure/revocation/review races, Storage object deletion and scheduling/restore, deployed cookie/proxy/tickets, legal necessity and processing approval. SQL/browser/API fixture evidence does not prove these services. Qualified review/operational availability must be established before real-data activation; official automatic providers remain deferred under free-only.
